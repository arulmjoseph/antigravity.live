How JP Associates Drives Measurable Financial Control

We operate not as distant commentators, but as embedded technical partners. Our four-phase deployment model ensures every accounting, tax, or operational mandate produces auditable, lasting results.

Initiate Engagement Diagnostic
Phase 01

Diagnostic & Baseline Audit

Exhaustive review of historical general ledgers, tax exposure, inventory reconciliations, and existing internal control bottlenecks.

Phase 02

System & Policy Architecture

Design of tailored SOP manuals, DOA matrices, chart of accounts restructuring, and ERP software configurations.

Phase 03

Implementation & Ongoing Control

Hands-on execution of daily bookkeeping, monthly closing, cash flow modeling, VAT/Tax filing, and credit control enforcement.

Phase 04

Statutory Audit Liaison & Board Review

Preparation of end-of-year audit schedules, management letter remediation, and executive financial reporting to the Board of Directors.
