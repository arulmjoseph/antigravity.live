Privacy policy

A plain-language explanation of what this tool processes, what it stores, and which infrastructure services are involved.

Effective: September 29, 2026
Short versionYour selected images and PDFs are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our application server. No account is required.

Files you select

Image & PDF Optimizer uses browser APIs to read and process files that you explicitly select. Processing is designed to occur on your device. The application does not intentionally transmit the contents of those files to its hosting server.

Information stored in your browser

The site may use local browser storage to remember interface preferences such as light or dark theme. This information remains in your browser unless you clear site data. The tool does not use this storage to build a personal profile.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps, and security or performance data. This technical data is governed by Vercel’s own privacy practices.

Third-party libraries and fonts

The application loads JSZip, PDF-Lib, Google Fonts, and related browser resources from public content-delivery infrastructure. Those providers may receive standard connection data when your browser requests a resource. They do not receive the contents of the files you select through the application.

Cookies, accounts, and payments

The application does not require an account, does not sell access, and does not intentionally set advertising cookies. Third-party hosting or content-delivery services may use essential security technologies according to their own policies.

Children’s privacy

This general-purpose utility is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or submission form.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the tool, its hosting, or its dependencies change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
