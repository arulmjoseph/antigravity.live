Frequently asked questions

Is this image and PDF optimizer free?

Yes. The tool is free to use and does not require an account or sign-up.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. File processing takes place locally in your browser, so your selected files stay on your device.

Which image formats are supported?

The tool accepts common formats including PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG, with modern output options for web use.

Can I optimize several files together?

Yes. Add multiple files, apply shared conversion settings, and download the optimized results together as a ZIP archive.
