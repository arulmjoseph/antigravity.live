Empowering Tomorrow's
Innovators With STEM

At AAS, STEM comes alive. Students of all ages engage in real-world, hands-on learning, exploring robotics, coding, engineering, and other cutting-edge technologies through creative, practical projects. Each experience sparks curiosity, builds problem-solving skills, and prepares students to thrive in a fast-changing world. By collaborating, experimenting, and innovating, students gain the confidence to turn ideas into solutions that make a meaningful impact. From the classroom to the real world, students apply their knowledge to solve challenges that matter.

Message from the Principal

It is a privilege to serve the American Academy School (AAS) community as the School Principal. I consider it an honor to have the opportunity to lead a school with such a longstanding history in the educational sector in Qatar.

The privilege of working with such a talented and dedicated faculty and staff, a tremendous student body, and a supportive network of parents and community members will enable AAS towards new success stories.

The teachers, staff, and administration at AAS are committed to providing high quality education, along with relevant extra-curricular opportunities, that will meet the individual needs and interests of all the students here at our Thumama campus.
