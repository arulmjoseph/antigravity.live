OUR ORIGIN STORY

Rooted in Tradition. Engineered for the Street.

"We didn't want to make another generic sneaker. We wanted a pair that carries the soul, fire, and rain of our home wherever you walk."

Kayron was born out of a desire to see authentic Indian regional culture represented in high-end streetwear. Each shoe uses a single, refined silhouette as a canvas, overlayed with color stories directly drawn from Kerala's ritual art, spice coasts, and monsoon rains.

Produced in strictly limited numerical drops to ensure zero material wastage and maximum quality control.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do Kayron sneakers fit? +
Kayron sneakers run true to standard UK shoe sizing. If you usually wear UK 8 in major athletic brands, pick UK 8. If you are between sizes, we recommend ordering 1 size up.
What is your shipping and return policy? +
We offer Free Express Shipping across all Indian pin codes. Deliveries typically arrive within 3-5 business days. We offer a 14-day hassle-free size exchange and return policy.
How should I care for the suede uppers? +
We recommend using a dry soft-bristle suede brush to gently remove dust. Avoid machine washing or submerging in water. A water-repellent suede spray is recommended before first wear.
