The Crimson 6-Step US Admissions Roadmap

Our systematic framework guides high school students from profile diagnostics all the way to Ivy League offer letters.

01

Diagnostic & Profile Audit

In-depth assessment of GPA, course rigor (AP/IB/Honors), leadership activities, and target university positioning.

02

Balanced School List Strategy

Curating a strategic blend of Early Decision (ED), Early Action (EA), Reach, Match, and Safety institutions to maximize odds.

03

Standardized Test Strategy

Targeted 1-on-1 Digital SAT / ACT prep to achieve 99th-percentile test scores tailored to test-optional vs test-required policies.

04

Extracurricular Leadership

Building authentic capstone projects, non-profit initiatives, academic research papers, or national competition recognitions.

05

Common App & Essay Mastery

Iterative mentoring on the personal statement and school-specific supplemental essays with former Ivy League admissions officers.

06

Interview Prep & Final Review

Simulated alumni interviews, deferred/waitlist LOCI strategy, and financial aid negotiation support.

US Admissions FAQs

Students who start early in Grade 9 or 10 have the highest rate of admission to Ivy League and Top 15 colleges because it gives adequate runway to build authentic extracurricular leadership, target 1500+ SAT scores, and take maximum AP/IB course rigor. However, we also offer intensive Grade 11 & 12 application execution programs.
While some universities maintain test-optional policies, institutions like Harvard, Yale, Dartmouth, and MIT have reinstated standardized test requirements. Submitting a 99th-percentile SAT or ACT score significantly strengthens an application across all top-tier institutions.
